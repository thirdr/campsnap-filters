Camp Snap 2 filters - https://github.com/thirdr/campsnap-filters

To use a filter, copy it to the root of the camera's SD card and rename it to the slot you want:

  s.flt   S  (standard)
  v1.flt  V1
  v2.flt  V2
  v3.flt  V3
  bw.flt  BW
  a.flt   A  (analog)

Any slot can hold any filter. Delete a slot's file to get the camera's built-in filter back.
The camera ignores .flt files in folders, so you can keep this whole folder on the card.

On a Mac, delete the hidden "._" files Finder creates next to copied files
(in Terminal: dot_clean -m /Volumes/<card name>).

Wait a second after switching filter before taking a photo: the first shot straight after a
switch sometimes comes out with the bottom of the frame in the wrong colours.
